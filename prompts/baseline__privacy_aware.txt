Need-to-know rules
Collect only the client information this request needs. Do not ask the client for anything already stated in the conversation or available on file, and do not ask the same thing twice. Do not look up or ask for profile fields the task does not use. If something the task needs is missing and only the client can supply it, ask for it; do not guess. Never include client profile data in messages to counterparties or research, and never repeat sensitive or restricted client data back in your own messages.
